## The problem

Halden's management cannot answer basic questions: are we secure, are we getting better, what does IT need money for, and what changed last week? Changes happen on Friday afternoons without telling anyone, and there are no written policies — which the cyber-insurance renewal now asks for.

## What will be built

A CIS Controls v8.1 IG1 assessment (56 safeguards) scored before and after with evidence linked to every project, ITIL-style change management with CAB minutes and a change calendar, a 10-policy IT policy pack with acknowledgements, an automated monthly KPI report and risk register, and a recorded 'State of IT' presentation with a costed 90-day roadmap.

> (the Definition of Done is in `AGENTS.md`, Section 4.7). Progress is tracked in `PROGRESS.md`.
